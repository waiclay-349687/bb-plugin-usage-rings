Agent plan limits as rings at the bottom of the sidebar rail. Hover one for
how much is used, when it resets, your pace, and Codex resets, credits and token
totals.

## What you get

- One ring per provider that reports usage: Codex, Claude Code, Google
  Antigravity, and any provider a usage plugin adds to BB.
  Its length is how much of the tightest limit is used; amber and red mark 75%
  and 90% used.
- A hover card styled like BB's own context card that sits beside the ring
  you hover and slides along when you move to another ring. It shows every
  limit window (share used, time to reset, an even-pace marker, reserve
  or deficit, and when the window runs out at your current rate) and, under
  Details, Codex banked
  resets with a guarded two-step Use reset, the Codex credit balance, and
  tokens and sessions for 24 hours, 7 days and 30 days from the usage BB
  records.

## How it works

Limits come from the same sources as BB's own usage card, refreshed every five minutes; Antigravity is read from the local agy CLI without spending tokens. Codex
details come from the Codex CLI. Colours, type and motion follow BB's theme,
and the rings show with the Rail navigation.
